1-1: ...的話 (dehuà)

(要是/如果) + Condition + 的話, ...

Usage

Expresses a conditional mood ("if..."). The clause before 的話 is the condition, and the clause after it is the result. 要是 (yàoshi) or 如果 (rúguǒ) can be used at the beginning for emphasis but are optional.

Examples (from materials)

  • 王: 寄到日本的航空信要貼多少錢郵票？
    李: 不超重的話, 十三塊。     (Bù chāozhòng dehuà, shísān kuài.) - If it's not overweight, thirteen dollars.
  • 要是下雨的話, 怎麼去王老師家？ (Yàoshi xià yǔ dehuà, zěnme qù Wáng lǎoshī jiā?) - If it rains, how do we get to Teacher Wang's house?
  • 如果這種洗衣機不錯的話, 我就買。 (Rúguǒ zhè zhǒng xǐyījī bùcuò dehuà, wǒ jiù mǎi.) - If this kind of washing machine is good, I'll buy it.

1-2: 每...就... (měi...jiù...)

每 + (Nu + M + N), 就 + V...

Usage

Shows a regularly repeated action. It means "every... then..." or "for each... then...". The 就 (jiù) can sometimes be omitted.

Examples (from materials)

  • 每超重十公克就加九塊錢。 (Měi chāozhòng shí gōngkè jiù jiā jiǔ kuài qián.) - For every ten grams overweight, (they) add nine dollars.
  • 王: 信超重的話, 要貼多少錢郵票？
    李: 每超重十公克加九塊。     (...Měi chāozhòng shí gōngkè jiā jiǔ kuài.) - ...For every ten grams overweight, add nine dollars.

1-3: 就 (jiù) vs. 才 (cái)

...就 + V + 了 (sooner than expected)
...才 + V (later than expected)

Usage

These adverbs indicate the speaker's perception of time.

  • 就 (jiù): Indicates an action happened sooner, faster, or easier than expected.
  • 才 (cái): Indicates an action happened later, slower, or with more difficulty than expected.

Examples (from materials)

  • 李: 他昨天來了。
    王: 你朋友還沒來嗎？ (comparing with "he arrived yesterday")     - (This implies "yesterday" was earlier than the speaker thought).
  • 李: 我半個小時以前就到了。 (Wǒ bàn ge xiǎoshí yǐqián jiù dào le.) - I arrived half an hour ago (sooner than expected).
  • 李: 五分鐘以前就走了。 (Wǔ fēnzhōng yǐqián jiù zǒu le.) - (He/She) left five minutes ago (implying this was relatively recent/quick).
  • 李: 念書是自己的事, 不能等老師說了你才看。 (Niànshū shì zìjǐ de shì, bùnéng děng lǎoshī shuōle nǐ cái kàn.) - Studying is your own business, you can't wait until *after* the teacher tells you to (only then) study.

2-1 & 3-2: 把 (bǎ) Sentence

S + (Adv/AV) + 把 + Object + V + Other Element (C)

Usage

The "disposal" 把 (bǎ) sentence emphasizes what happens to a specific, definite object. The verb cannot be simple; it must have an "other element" (like a complement, 了, 給, 在, etc.) to show the result of the disposal.

Use 把 when you are:

  1. Changing the object's location: 我把書放在桌子上｡ (I put the book on the table.)
  2. Giving the object to someone: 請把身分證給我｡ (Please give me the ID card.)
  3. Changing the object's status: 我把桌子擦乾淨｡ (I wiped the table clean.)
  4. Making the object "disappear" (e.g., eating, selling): 我把蛋糕吃了｡ (I ate the cake.)

Note: Verbs like 喜歡 (like), 知道 (know), 有 (have), or 是 (to be) cannot be used with 把.

Examples (from materials)

  • 李: 請把通知單給我。 (Qǐng bǎ tōngzhīdān gěi wǒ.) - Please give me the notification slip.
  • 李: 噢, 我把(那個包子)吃了。 (Ō, wǒ bǎ (nèige bāozi) chī le.) - Oh, I ate (that bun).
  • 王: 為什麼要把身分證影本給他們？
    李: 因為旅行社要我把身分證影本給他們。     (Yīnwèi lǚxíngshè yào wǒ bǎ shēnfènzhèng yǐngběn gěi tāmen.)

2-2: V 在 (V zài)

V + 在 + Location

Usage

Indicates the resulting location of an action. The object (if any) comes before the verb, often in a 把 sentence, or is implied.

Used with:

  • Posture Verbs: 坐 (sit), 站 (stand), 睡 (sleep), 住 (live), etc.
  • Displacement Verbs: 放 (put), 留 (leave), 停 (park), 掛 (hang), 貼 (stick), etc.

Examples (from materials)

  • 李: 要。請簽在這裡。 (Yào. Qǐng qiān zài zhèlǐ.) - Yes. Please sign here.
  • A: 我的杯子呢？ B: B: 你的杯子放在桌子上。 (Nǐ de bēizi fàng zài zhuōzi shàng.) - Your cup is placed on the table.

3-1: V 給 (V gěi)

V + 給 + Indirect Object (Person)

Usage

Indicates the recipient (the person "given" the action). The structure is often S + V + O + 給 + Person or S + 把 + O + V + 給 + Person.

Examples (from materials)

  • 他從日本回來, 帶給我一本書。 (Tā cóng Rìběn huílái, dài gěi wǒ yī běn shū.) - He came back from Japan and brought me a book.
  • 買書的錢, 應該 ______ 你還是王小姐？ (Mǎi shū de qián, yīnggāi ______ nǐ háishì Wáng xiǎojiě?)

3-3: 幫 (bāng)

N1 + 幫 + N2 + V(O)

Usage

N1 helps N2 perform an action. 幫 (bāng) means "to help" or "for".

Examples (from materials)

  • 李: 沒關係。我有傳真機, 可以幫你傳真。 (Méi guānxì. Wǒ yǒu chuánzhēnjī, kěyǐ bāng nǐ chuánzhēn.) - No problem. I have a fax machine, I can help you fax.
  • 王: 我的車有一點問題。
    李: 你明天把 ______ 。我幫你看看。     (Nǐ míngtiān bǎ ______. Wǒ bāng nǐ kànkan.)

3-4: V 來 / V 去 (V lái / V qù)

(DV) + V + 來 / 去

Usage

A directional complement that indicates the direction of an action relative to the speaker.

  • 來 (lái): Action moves *towards* the speaker. (e.g., 拿來 ná lái - bring here)
  • 去 (qù): Action moves *away* from the speaker. (e.g., 拿去 ná qù - take there)

This can also act as the complement in a 把 sentence.

Examples (from materials)

  • 李: 請把身分證影本拿來。 (Qǐng bǎ shēnfènzhèng yǐngběn ná lái.) - Please bring the ID photocopy here.
  • 李: 你可以先把 ______ 。自己帶太重了。 (寄去) (Nǐ kěyǐ xiān bǎ ______ . Zìjǐ dài tài zhòng le.)

3-5: 呢 (ne)

QW / A-not-A ... + 呢?

Usage

A question particle placed at the end of a question-word (QW) sentence or an "A-not-A" question. It does not go with 嗎 (ma).

It conveys a tone of wonder or confusion, or simply softens the inquisitive tone of the question.

Examples (from materials)

  • 你買不買日本車, 也不買美國車, 那 ______？ (Nǐ mǎi bù mǎi Rìběn chē, yě bù mǎi Měiguó chē, nà ______?)
  • 你那通電話不是打給錢書宣, 也不是打給張台生, 那是 ______？ (Nǐ nà tōng diànhuà bùshì dǎ gěi Qián Shūxuān, yě bùshì dǎ gěi Zhāng Táishēng, nà shì ______?)
